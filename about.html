About AiLSA Studio

Built in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, Malaysia 🇲🇾

AiLSA Studio is a creative toolkit for making AI-powered videos, images, UGC-style content, and ads — all in one place. Whether you're creating YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok clips, or product ads, our goal is to make AI video creation fast and simple.

Our mission: To give creators everywhere an easy, all-in-one studio for turning ideas into engaging short-form video — no editing experience required.

What We Offer

  • Text to Video — turn a prompt into a vertical video clip
  • Text to Image — generate images from text (free)
  • AI Scriptwriter — hooks, scripts, and shot lists
  • Voiceover & Lip Sync — AI voices and avatars
  • Auto Captions — viral caption styles
  • Marketing / Ad Cloner — ad hooks and variants

How It Works

Type a prompt, pick a model, and hit generate. We route your request to AI model providers (like fal.ai) to create the content. In our current free preview, image generation is fully free and video returns a sample preview.

Our Values

  • Creator-first: Built for people making short-form content.
  • Simple: No steep learning curve — just create.
  • Responsible: We encourage lawful, original content and respect for others' rights.
