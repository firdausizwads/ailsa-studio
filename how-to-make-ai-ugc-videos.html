How to Make AI UGC Videos That Convert

AiLSA Studio • 8 min read

User-generated content (UGC) style ads are everywhere — and for good reason. They feel authentic, relatable, and convert well for e-commerce and app brands. The best part? With AI, you can create realistic UGC-style videos in minutes, without hiring a creator.

What Makes a UGC Video "Convert"?

High-converting UGC usually has three things:

  1. A strong hook in the first 3 seconds (a bold claim, a question, or a before/after).
  2. Authentic delivery — handheld camera feel, natural light, a real-person tone.
  3. A clear call to action at the end (link in bio, "try it free", "use code X").

Step 1: Write a Great Prompt

Your AI video prompt is everything. Describe the subject, setting, camera movement, mood, and the on-screen action in detail. Here's a template:

"A woman in her late 20s unboxes a skincare serum in a bright bathroom, handheld camera, natural morning light, excited genuine expression, 9:16 vertical, UGC style, no watermark"

Step 2: Pick the Right Format

For social ads, use 9:16 vertical and keep clips short (5–10 seconds). Short clips are faster to generate and keep viewer attention.

Step 3: Add Captions & a CTA

Most people watch with sound off, so burn-in captions help a lot. Finish with a clear call to action on screen.

Pro tip: Generate 3–5 variations of the same hook, then split-test which one gets the most clicks. AI makes iteration cheap and fast.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Prompts that are too vague ("make a cool ad") → results are generic.
  • Ignoring aspect ratio → clips look bad on social feeds.
  • No hook in the first seconds → viewers scroll past.
