How to Start a Faceless YouTube Shorts Channel With AI

AiLSA Studio • 9 min read

Faceless YouTube Shorts channels are one of the most popular ways to earn with AI video. You never show your face, but you can still build an audience and get monetized. Here's the exact system.

Step 1: Choose a Niche

Pick something you can produce consistently. Winning niches for faceless shorts:

  • Motivation & self-improvement
  • Finance tips & money habits
  • History & "did you know" facts
  • Tech & AI news
  • Stories / true crime / mysteries

Step 2: Build a Content Workflow

  1. Script — a strong 20–40 second hook-driven script.
  2. Visuals — generate AI video clips (9:16 vertical) that match each line.
  3. Voiceover — AI voice, energetic and clear.
  4. Captions — bold captions since most watch on mute.
  5. Edit — combine clips, captions, and voiceover.

Step 3: Post Consistently

Shorts reward consistency. Aim for 1 Short per day for 30 days. Track which topics get the most views and double down.

Step 4: Get Monetized

YouTube monetizes channels that hit 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days for Shorts). Keep posting and let the algorithm find your audience.

Pro tip: Use AiLSA Studio's Text to Video and Auto Captions to produce a whole Short quickly. The faster you can publish, the faster you learn what works.

Common Mistakes

  • Jumping between niches (confuses the algorithm).
  • No hook in the first 3 seconds.
  • Quitting before 30 uploads.
