10 AI Video Prompts for YouTube Shorts & Reels

AiLSA Studio • 6 min read

Here are copy-paste-ready prompts you can drop into the AiLSA Studio Text to Video tool. Just replace the bracketed part with your own product or topic.

  1. UGC Skincare Unboxing 
    A woman unboxes a [product] in a bright bathroom, handheld camera, natural light, excited genuine reaction, 9:16 vertical
  2. Product Ad — Floating Object 
    A [product] floats and rotates slowly in a neon studio, particles, commercial lighting, slow motion, cinematic
  3. AI Avatar Explainer 
    An AI avatar talking to camera explaining [topic] in 15 seconds, lip sync, clean studio background, professional
  4. Drone Landscape (Kota Kinabalu) 
    Epic drone shot over islands at sunset, turquoise water, cinematic, 8k, smooth camera movement
  5. Fitness Before/After 
    Transition from tired to energetic person doing a workout, morning light, motivational mood, 9:16
  6. Food & Recipe Close-up 
    Close-up of pouring [sauce/drink] over food, steam rising, warm lighting, appetizing, macro shot, vertical
  7. Faceless Finance Tips 
    A faceless video showing money growing, coins and graphs, dark background with neon green accents, 9:16
  8. Travel Destination Tease 
    Fast cinematic montage of [city/beach], quick cuts, vibrant colors, travel vlog style, vertical
  9. AI Ad — Before/After Result 
    Split screen before and after using [product], transformation effect, clear improvement, commercial quality
  10. Motivational Quote Loop 
    Animated bold typography of a motivational quote over a slow abstract background, minimal, premium feel, vertical

Tips for Better Results

  • Add "9:16 vertical, no watermark" to prompts meant for Shorts/Reels.
  • Keep subjects and actions specific — vague prompts give generic results.
  • Mention lighting and mood (natural light, warm, cinematic) for better realism.
  • For ads, describe the end result and a clear visual of the product.
Pro tip: Save your best prompts and reuse them with small variations. The top creators iterate on proven hooks rather than starting from scratch.
