Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

AiLSA Studio ("we", "us", "our") provides this website and the tools on it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Summary: We collect as little data as possible. If you just browse the site, we don't collect any personal information. Any content you generate stays on your device or is handled by our AI providers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require an account to use the site. We do not collect your name, email, or payment information unless you choose to provide it.

  • Content you enter: The prompts and text you type are sent to our AI model providers (such as fal.ai) solely to generate the content you request. We do not sell this content.
  • Anonymous usage data: Our hosting provider (Vercel) may collect basic, non-identifying analytics such as pages viewed, referring site, and approximate location, to help keep the site working and improve it.
  • Cookies: We may use cookies from advertising partners (see Section 3) and from our hosting/analytics provider.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the site.
  • To generate the content you request through our AI providers.
  • To display advertisements (if you consent).

3. Advertising & Cookies

We may display third-party advertising. Ad partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com).

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party providers for hosting (Vercel) and AI generation (fal.ai, and others). These providers process your data under their own privacy policies to deliver the service. Please review their policies for details.

5. Data Retention & Security

We do not store your prompts or generated content on our own servers. Generated files are returned to you and are not retained by us. We take reasonable measures to protect any data transmitted through the site.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads as described in Section 3.
  • Contact us if you have questions about your data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
